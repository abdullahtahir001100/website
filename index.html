F.A.Q

What is InkByHand?

A specialized studio where traditional calligraphy meets modern digital architecture. We focus on identity through motion and ink.

Process Duration?

Depending on complexity, a bespoke brand mark typically requires 2 to 4 weeks of exploration and refinement.

Digital Assets?

All hand-inked works are high-fidelity scanned and delivered in vector formats (SVG, AI) for infinite scalability.

Global Reach?

While based in Sargodha, our systems and collaborations are built for international brand standards.

Custom Scripts?

Yes. We develop custom alphabet sets and typefaces based on specific manual handwriting styles.

The Philosophy?

Zero radius. Zero shadows. Just the raw, sharp elegance of precision craftsmanship.
